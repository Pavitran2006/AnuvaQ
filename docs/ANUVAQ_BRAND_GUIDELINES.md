# AnuvaQ — Official Brand & Visual Identity Guidelines

**Document Version**: 2.2.0  
**Product Title**: **AnuvaQ — Interactive Quantum Computing & Noise Simulation Platform**  
**Tagline**: *"Explore Quantum Computing Through Interactive Simulation"*  
**Brand Philosophy**: *"From the smallest origin to infinite quantum possibilities."*

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Name Origin

The name **AnuvaQ** encapsulates the fundamental journey of quantum scientific discovery and technological computation:

1. **"Anu" (अणु / Smallest Origin)**:
   Represents the fundamental, elementary origin point—the single qubit state vector, scalar amplitude, or seed idea that initiates all quantum physics.
2. **"Q" (Quantum & Computation)**:
   Represents quantum state space, superposition, Kraus operator dynamics, complex linear algebra, and computational power.
3. **The Complete Narrative Arc**:
   **Origin ($\text{Anu}$) $\longrightarrow$ Quantum Possibilities ($|\psi\rangle$) $\longrightarrow$ Kraus Noise Simulation $\longrightarrow$ High-Performance Computation $\longrightarrow$ Real-World Impact**.

## 2. Logo Geometry & Symbolic Components

The custom **A + Q** symbol is a unified, geometric vector mark engineered specifically for scientific computing interfaces.

```
       ✦  [Curiosity Spark]
       /\
      /  \  ▪ ▪ ▪ [Particle Transition]
     / |\ \
    /  | \ \
   /---●---\  [Quantum Nucleus Core]
  /    |    \
 (     |     ) [Integrated Q Loop & Quantum Orbit]
  \____|____/
```

### Component Breakdown & Meaning
1. **The 'A' Structure**:
   The primary structural apex. Represents architectural stability, algorithmic precision, and the origin ($\text{Anu}$).
2. **The Integrated 'Q' Loop**:
   The right leg of the 'A' sweeps fluidly into a terminal loop, forming the letter 'Q'. Represents the transition from scalar states to full quantum circuit operation.
3. **Quantum Nucleus Core**:
   A vibrant cyan particle (`#38bdf8`) located at the crossbar intersection. Represents the initialized $|0\dots0\rangle$ state vector core.
4. **Quantum Orbit**:
   An elliptical vector path passing through the symbol at $-30^\circ$. Represents superposition, phase rotation, and quantum exploration.
5. **Particle Transition**:
   A subtle 3-stage square pixel breakup at the upper-right apex. Represents quantum physics translating into digital computation and software execution.
6. **Curiosity Spark**:
   A 4-point star at the apex of the 'A'. Represents the fundamental question driving scientific inquiry.

## 4. Typography & Wordmark Specs

- **Wordmark**: `AnuvaQ`
- **Primary Typeface**: `Inter` (UI elements, headings, controls)
- **Monospace Typeface**: `JetBrains Mono` (OpenQASM code, matrix vectors, gate labels, JSON states)
- **Wordmark Style**: Bold font-mono with text-slate-100 body and `#06B6D4` cyan accent on the letter **Q**.
- **Version Tag**: `v2.2` (Rendered inside a subtle cyan border badge `bg-quantum-cyan/10 border-quantum-cyan/30`).

## 5. Favicon & Tab Icon Guidelines

- **Standalone SVG**: Located at `/favicon.svg` and `/assets/favicon.svg`.
- **Target Sizes**: Scalability optimized down to $16 \times 16$ and $32 \times 32$ pixels.
- **Design Rule**: The favicon displays the unified A+Q symbol, Quantum Nucleus, and Orbit on a dark tile (`#070A12` with rounded corners). The wordmark `AnuvaQ` is excluded from favicon usage to maintain legibility.

---

## 6. Correct vs. Incorrect Usage Examples

###  Correct Usage
- Displaying the full `AnuvaQLogo` component on Navbar, Hero, Auth views, and Footer.
- Using `#06B6D4` cyan and `#3B82F6` blue for active quantum elements.
- Maintaining clean margin spacing around the A+Q symbol.
- Using vector SVG for infinite resolution scaling.

### ❌ Incorrect Usage
- Replacing the A+Q symbol with generic stock atom illustrations.
- Adding heavy neon drop shadows or blinding glows around the logo.
- Splitting "Anuva" and "Q" into separate unlinked text blocks.
- Using bright white light backgrounds behind the primary logo.
